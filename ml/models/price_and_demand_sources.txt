DATA PROVENANCE — real data used for the ML modules
====================================================

Price prediction:
  Source: FAOSTAT Producer Prices (domain PP), Area = Kenya
  File: ml/data/fao_kenya_prices.csv
  Real official annual producer prices, 2010-2024, 18 crops, USD/tonne
  Converted to KES/kg for marketplace realism (1 USD approx 130 KES)

Demand forecasting:
  Source: FAOSTAT Crops and livestock products (domain QCL), Area = Kenya
  File: ml/data/fao_kenya_production.csv
  Real official annual production quantities, 2010-2024, 12 crops, tonnes

Fraud detection:
  Operates on transaction structure (synthetic transaction dataset).
  No fabricated fraud labels — anomalies detected statistically.

Collaborative filtering:
  Synthetic buyer-farmer transaction dataset (no public real equivalent
  exists). Clearly labelled as synthetic throughout.
